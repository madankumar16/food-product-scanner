Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ site.policy_date }}

{{ site.name }} is run by {{ site.owner or 'its operator' }} ("we"). We built it to work without collecting personal data.

No accounts, no tracking

There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

What happens when you scan

  • Scanned codes (the number or text inside a barcode or QR code) are sent to our server. Product and book numbers are looked up in the free Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Pet Food Facts, Open Products Facts and Open Library databases. Other codes, such as links, payment requests or Wi-Fi details, are only decoded and are never passed on or stored. Our server never opens links from codes. Our server does not pass your IP address to these databases.
  • Product photos and book covers in a result are loaded by your browser directly from the database's image server (images.openfoodfacts.org and similar, or covers.openlibrary.org). Like any website, that server sees your IP address when it sends the picture.
  • Photos are checked on our server for a barcode or QR code and then discarded. We don't save them.
    • {% if ai_enabled %}
  • Label reading: if no barcode is found, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini service to read the label text. Google processes it under its own terms. Don't photograph anything personal.
    • {% endif %}
  • Camera: the live scanner reads codes entirely in your browser. No video is sent to us.

Your history and deleting your data

Recent scans and saved items are stored only on your device: in your browser on the website, and on your phone in the app. We never receive them.

To delete them, use Delete my scans on the Settings page (or Settings in the app), or clear this site's data in your browser. Uninstalling the app deletes its data too. Because we hold nothing else about you, there is nothing more to request; if you still want to ask, use the contact details below.

The mobile app

The app works like the website: scanned codes and photos go to the same server and are handled as described above. The camera is used only while the Home tab is open and you have pressed Scan. PDF reports are created on our server, sent to your phone and not kept. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.

Server logs

Our host keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for security, and we briefly count requests per IP to stop abuse. These are not used for anything else.

Children

Because we don't collect personal data, no age check is needed. Parents may still want to supervise young children online.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws, you can ask what we hold about you. In practice, that's nothing beyond short-lived server logs.
