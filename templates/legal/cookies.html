Cookie Policy

Last updated {{ site.policy_date }}

{{ site.name }} sets no cookies of any kind: no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Why there is no "accept cookies" choice

Consent is needed for cookies or storage that aren't essential, such as analytics or advertising. We use none. The only thing stored is the history you create yourself, which the site needs to show it to you. The notice at the bottom of the page tells you this once.

Browser storage

Your browser's local storage keeps, on your device only:

  • your recent scans and saved items (up to 50), and
  • that you closed the notice about this policy.

This is never sent to us. Delete it with Delete my scans on the Settings page, or by clearing your browser's site data.
